I haven't finished many posts lately, but I have been deploying small style updates the last few weeks.

![Screenshot of this website homepage as of now](/assets/2026/07/homepage-screenshot.png "Screenshot of this website homepage as of now")
<p class="caption" markdown="1">I had updated the background of the header/menu a while ago with this vector leaf pattern, and changed the highlight colors from a green palette to a pink one. Then I changed it to cover the whole homepage.</p>

![Screenshot of a post page on this website as of now](/assets/2026/07/post-screenshot.png "Screenshot of a post page on this website as of now")
<p class="caption" markdown="1">It was actually the pink in [this image](/2026/06/20/it-may-not-be-much-light-but-it-beats-the-darkness/) that inspired the color change. I've removed drop-shadows from images, as they did not look as good in dark mode. I rarely use dark mode (bad eyes) so it sometimes gets neglected.</p>

![Screenshot of a post page on this website as of now](/assets/2026/07/aside-screenshot.png "Screenshot of a post page on this website as of now")
<p class="caption" markdown="1">I've also updated the aside element with the same striped background I've had on the menu items for a while. The previous iteration was a vector OS window frame, which was a little heavy-handed.</p>

![Screenshot of the post footer and page footer on this website as of now](/assets/2026/07/footer-screenshot.png "Screenshot of the post footer and page footer on this website as of now")
<p class="caption" markdown="1">I tweaked the post and page footers as well.</p>

I added more links the [blogroll](/blogroll) and [about](/about) pages. I used the robot to improve the build times of the site, mostly via fixing up my hand-written plugins and liquid tags, something I immediately added a disclaimer about to the top of [colophon](/colophon). I'm including the screenshots above mostly for the benefit of future-me, so he does not have to go to the Internet Archive to remember what the heck I'm talking about.

Screenshots via [Equinox](https://equinox.netigen.com/), which I discovered today via [Nick Simson's Notes](https://www.nicksimson.com/notes/), who I discovered on today's [Homebrew Website Club - Writing Edition](https://events.indieweb.org/2026/07/homebrew-website-club-writing-edition-m2NVbVuF8wqT), which was delightful as always; I'm happy to have noticed it was happening (just a couple of days ago); thanks again to [James](https://jamesg.blog/) for hosting. Both the work done behind the scenes on our sites and meta-blogging were mentioned/discussed, which might have motivated me to post this.

I've also been going through a large backlog of old posts not included in this site's archive, which results in posts like [Over the Rhine Record Review, 2001](/2026/06/14/over-the-rhine-record-review-2001/) and [Art for Art's Sake](/2026/05/20/art-for-arts-sake/), and random almost-footnotes like in [It May Not Be Much Light but it Beats the Darkness](/2026/06/20/it-may-not-be-much-light-but-it-beats-the-darkness/) pointing to [posts I'm injecting straight into the archive](/2021/09/02/The-Radio-Keeps-Playing-All-the-Usual/).
