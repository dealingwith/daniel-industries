🪨 "Hey."

🌳 "Hey."

🪨 "Decent morning."

🌳 "Yep."

🪨 "Hell of a storm last night."

🌳 "Yep."

🪨 "Thought I was going to get washed away there for a minute."

🌳 "Oh yeah?"

🪨 "Yeah. At least you have roots. I just have weight."

🌳 "Well at least you have that."

🪨 "That water, though, it has weight."

🌳 "I like the water."

🪨 "You would."

🌳 "Tastes good."

🪨 "Gets me wet."

🌳 "Well, the sun's out now."

🪨 "Yep. Feels good."

🌳 "Can get a little hot."

🪨 "Have you seen that construction?"

🌳 "Oh yeah, I've seen it for a while. It keeps moving this way."

🪨 "Oh, yeah? I just noticed it. I guess you saw it sooner from up there."

🌳 "Yep."

🪨 "What are they building?"

🌳 "Not sure. But they're moving a lot of dirt."

🪨 "I wonder if my cousin has been affected."

🌳 "What's his name? I'll ask if anyone has seen him."

🪨 "Steve. Steve the stone."

🌳 "Ok, I'll put the word out."

🪨 "Hey what's the noise?"

🌳 "Something's coming."

🪨 "What's it look like?"

🌳 "Yellow."

🪨 "It's loud!"

🌳 "Yeah, I don't like the looks of this."

<aside markdown="1">
I am searching for information about our last-hired tree trimming service. Instead I found this silly bit I wrote while participating in a daily writing community in 2019. I lightly edited it (didn't like the last few lines, they're gone now) and added the emojis, as even I had a hard time tracking which character was saying what by about halfway down.
